Welcome to ChemSim MUST

ChemSim MUST is the virtual chemistry laboratory of the Department of Applied Sciences, College of Science and Technical Education, Mbeya University of Science and Technology. It lets Science Laboratory Technology students rehearse the NSLT 07406 practicals step by step, with the same procedures, reagents and safety discipline expected at the physical bench.

Organic Chemistry

Extraction, synthesis, chromatography, distillation and saponification practicals.

Inorganic & Complexes

Coordination compounds, double salts, qualitative cation analysis and gravimetry.

Analytical & Titration

Colorimetry, spectrophotometry, redox, complexometric and conductometric titrations.

MUST laboratory safety rules

Follow these COSTE Applied Sciences protocols before every virtual or physical practical.

1
Lab coats at all timesWear a fastened laboratory coat from entry to exit. Bare arms are not permitted at the MUST bench.
2
Safety goggles requiredEye protection stays on during heating, titration, distillation and any transfer of reagents.
3
No eating or drinkingFood, drinks and cosmetics are forbidden in the laboratory to prevent chemical ingestion.
4
Proper pipetting onlyUse a pipette filler or bulb. Mouth pipetting is strictly prohibited under MUST safety policy.
5
Acid into waterAlways add acid to water, slowly and with stirring. Never add water to concentrated acid.
